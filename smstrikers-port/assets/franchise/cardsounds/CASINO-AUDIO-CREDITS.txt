Quest 3 Strikers - the STRIKERZ casino machine's audio (casino_win_*, casino_lose_*, casino_coin*, casino_chips_*,
casino_roulette_ball_roll). 25 clips are CC0 (public domain); 3 are CC-BY and credited below. Each was trimmed,
faded, normalized and converted to 16-bit 32 kHz mono. The machine's mechanical sounds (casino_pull, _reels, _stop,
_tease, _wheel, _nomore, _lever, _engage, _chip) were made for the game in-house (numpy synthesis).
